# Glossary (English / German)

The terms of the documentation and the code, with their German
counterparts and their meaning in groundfield. Use it to look up a term of
the package or of the numerics, or to find the English word for a German
one ([German → English](#german-english) at the end). The documentation
and the code use English terms; the German terms are those of
DIN EN 50522 (VDE 0101-2), DIN 18014 and the German network practice.
Names of releases up to 0.15.0 that were German (`kvs`, `OrtsnetzLayout`,
…) are listed under [Deprecations](deprecations.md).

## Soil and fields

| English | German | Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| soil resistivity | spezifischer Erdwiderstand | $\rho$, $\rho_E$ | Ω·m; `rho`, `rho_1`, `rho_2` |
| two-layer soil | Zweischichtboden | | upper layer $\rho_1$, thickness $h_1$, half-space $\rho_2$ |
| layer thickness | Schichtdicke | $h_1$ | m |
| reflection coefficient | Reflexionsfaktor | $K$ | $(\rho_2 - \rho_1)/(\rho_2 + \rho_1)$ |
| effective resistivity | effektiver spezifischer Erdwiderstand | $\rho_\text{eff}$ | Ω·m; one resistivity that stands for a two-layer soil: the `rho` of a groundinsight bus and of a BusType in layered soil (`effective_resistivity`, see below) |
| remote earth | Bezugserde | | potential reference, $\varphi \to 0$ far away |
| soil surface | Erdoberfläche | $z = 0$ | depth $z$ positive downwards |
| skin depth (in the soil) | Eindringtiefe | $\delta$ | $\sqrt{2\rho/(\omega\mu_0)}$ |
| earth return | Erdrückleitung | | path of a current through the soil back to its source; enters the inductive coupling |
| mutual inductance | Gegeninduktivität | $M$ | Neumann integral between two conductor segments |

The effective resistivity is $\rho_\text{eff} = \rho_1^{1-w}\rho_2^{w}$, a
weighted geometric mean of the two layers, with the weight
$w = w_0 (h_1/10\ \mathrm{m})^{-\beta}$ limited to $[0, 1]$: the thinner the
top layer, the more the lower layer counts. The weights are calibrated per
electrode class (AP1 calibration $w_0 = 0.13$, $\beta = 0.8$ by default;
presets `"station"` and `"lv_group_<g>_p<pp>"` in
`EFFECTIVE_RESISTIVITY_PRESETS`); for homogeneous soil
$\rho_\text{eff} = \rho$.

## Quantities

| English | German | Symbol | Meaning in groundfield |
|---|---|---|---|
| earth potential rise (EPR) | Erdungsspannung | $U_E$ | potential of an earthing system against remote earth |
| grounding impedance, earthing impedance | Erdungsimpedanz | $Z_E$ | $U_E / I$; three variants below |
| input impedance | Eingangsimpedanz | $Z_\text{in}$ | $(\varphi_a - \varphi_r)/I_s$ at a source: what an earth tester or a fault sees (`input_impedance`) |
| cluster impedance | Erdungsimpedanz des Erderverbunds | $Z_c$ | cluster potential over the current the cluster leaks itself (`cluster_impedance`) |
| electrode impedance | Erdungsimpedanz eines Erders | | cluster potential over one electrode's leakage current (`grounding_impedance`) |
| grounding resistance, spreading resistance | Erdungswiderstand, Ausbreitungswiderstand | $R_E$, $R_A$ | real part of the grounding impedance at low frequency |
| mutual grounding impedance | Koppelimpedanz | $Z_{ij}$ | potential of cluster $i$ per ampere leaking from cluster $j$ (`solve_mutual_matrix`) |
| touch voltage | Berührungsspannung | $U_T$ | potential of the touched part minus the surface potential 1 m from it (`touch_voltage_at_part`; `touch_voltage` with the electrode as the touched part) |
| touch point | Berührungspunkt | | an accessible metal part above ground bonded to the earthing system: station housing or door, mast, fence, cable cabinet, service connection box (`touch_voltage_at_part`) |
| transferred potential | Potentialverschleppung | | potential of an earthing system carried by a connected conductor (cable screen, PEN, pipe) into an area of low potential rise (EN 50522, 3.6; `touch_voltage_at_part(bonded_to=<junction>)`) |
| prospective touch voltage | Leerlauf-Berührungsspannung (prospektive B.) | $U_{vT}$ | open-circuit touch voltage, before body and footwear resistances (EN 50522, Annex B) |
| permissible touch voltage | zulässige Berührungsspannung | $U_{Tp}$ | EN 50522 Figure 8 / Table B.4 up to 10 s of fault duration, 80 V beyond |
| permissible prospective touch voltage | zulässige Leerlauf-Berührungsspannung | $U_{vTp}$ | $U_{Tp}$ plus the drop of the body current over hand, footwear and surface-layer resistances (EN 50522, Annex B; `permissible_prospective_touch_voltage_en50522`) |
| touch-voltage envelope | | | touch voltages on a ring of directions 1 m outside an electrode's footprint, a proxy where the touched parts are not known (`touch_voltage_envelope`) |
| mesh voltage | Maschenspannung (IEEE Std 80) | $E_m$ | the largest touch voltage inside a mesh of a grid, near the mesh centre (`touch_voltage_envelope(interior="meshes")`) |
| step voltage | Schrittspannung | $U_S$ | surface potential difference over one step of 1 m (`step_voltage`) |
| fault duration | Fehlerdauer | $t_F$ | s |
| current sharing | Stromaufteilung | | currents of the electrodes and conductors (`cluster_current_balance`) |
| split factor | Aufteilungsfaktor | $s$ | share of a source current that leaves the feeding cluster through the soil (`split_factor`) |
| reduction factor | Reduktionsfaktor | $r$ | share of an earth-fault current that returns through the soil instead of cable screens and earth wires (inductive; not computed) |
| rho-f model | ρ-f-Modell, ρ-f-Funktion | $Z(\rho, f)$ | reduced model of an earthing system over resistivity and frequency, exported to groundinsight |
| characteristic matrix | charakteristische Matrix | $(\mathbf{Y}_L + \mathbf{Y}_G)\,\mathbf{u} = \mathbf{i}$ | nodal description of an LV network: line admittances plus the inverse of the mutual grounding-impedance matrix |

## Terms of the package

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `World` | the physical model: soil, electrodes, junctions, conductors, sources |
| `Engine` | the numerics: backend, frequencies, segment length, kernel, earth-return model |
| `FieldResult` | the result of a solve: potentials, currents and impedances per frequency, point sources for the field, metadata |
| backend, engine | a registered numerical method (`image`, `image_2layer`, `mom`, `mom_sommerfeld`, `fem`); see [choosing an engine](../explanation/engines/index.md) |
| effective backend | the backend that actually ran: `image` runs `image_2layer` for a two-layer soil (`metadata["effective_backend"]`) |
| deprecated alias | an old backend name that runs another backend: `cim` runs `image`, `bem` runs `mom`; comparing an alias with its target checks nothing |
| segment | straight piece of a discretised wire, at most `segment_length` long |
| straight wire | a straight run of an electrode: a rod, a strip, a wire line of a grid, an arm of a star, a side of a polyline; carries one unknown current in the image family |
| owner | a straight wire (per soil layer it reaches) that carries one unknown current in the image family (user decisions Q30, Q44) |
| BusType | groundinsight's model of a network node with an impedance formula in `f` and `rho` (for layered soil the effective resistivity $\rho_\text{eff}$) |

## Methods and numerics

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Green's function | the potential of a unit point current in the layered soil; the engines differ in how they evaluate it |
| image family | the engines `image` and `image_2layer`: closed-form Green's functions (air image, Tagg/Sunde series) and one uniform current per straight wire |
| `mom` family | the engines `mom` and `mom_sommerfeld`: one current per segment, point matching |
| image series, Tagg/Sunde series | the two-layer Green's function as a sum of mirror images whose strengths are powers of the reflection coefficient $K$ |
| Sommerfeld integral | the layered Green's function as an integral over the horizontal wavenumber; evaluated by quadrature in `mom_sommerfeld` and for electrodes through a layer interface |
| cross-layer path | the Sommerfeld kernel that `image_2layer` and `mom` use as soon as an electrode reaches the layer interface (ADR-0007) |
| canonical layer stack | the soil after dropping zero-thickness layers and merging equal neighbours; a `TwoLayerSoil(ρ, ρ, h)` is solved as homogeneous |
| uniform-current ansatz | the current model of the image family: one leakage current per unit length per straight wire; impedance-grade, not field-grade next to grids and stars |
| point matching | the testing of the `mom` family: one pulse current per segment, the potential matched at every segment midpoint (collocation) |
| source kernel | how a segment's current enters the Green's function: `"line"` (exact uniform line source, the default of the image family) or `"point"` (midpoint point source, always in the `mom` family) |
| reaction matrix, multiport matrix | the matrix that turns the leakage currents of segments or wires into their potentials; reduced per cluster it is the mutual grounding-impedance matrix |
| transfer impedance | the mutual grounding impedance $Z_{ij}$ of two different clusters: the off-diagonal entries of the multiport matrix |
| earth-return model | how the soil enters the inductance of conductors: `sommerfeld` (default), `carson_series` (long parallel lines), `perfect_mirror` (diagnostic baseline) |
| Neumann inductance | the partial (self and mutual) inductance of conductor segments from the Neumann integral |
| skin effect | the current displacement inside a solid round conductor at higher frequency (`resistance_model="skin"`) |
| vector fit | a rational fit of a frequency response (poles and residues), the alternative to the standard rho-f form (`vector_fit`) |
| Richardson extrapolation | the extrapolation of `fem` over two nested mesh levels; the difference of the extrapolated value to the finer level is its error estimate (`metadata["fem_error_estimate"]`) |
